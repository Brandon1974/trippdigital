What You Get — 8 Sections

✓ Cycle at a Glance / Roadmap

Cycle type (IVF / IUI / FET / Other), clinic and care team, cycle start date, stimulation, retrieval, transfer and test key dates, a highlighted trigger-shot panel (date, exact time and medication name), roadmap notes, and a live roadmap summary card — autosaves as you type

Medication name, dose exactly as prescribed on your clinic paperwork, time, method, start and end dates and notes — add, edit or delete entries, check each dose off as done with the time recorded, and undo if you tap by mistake. Your trigger shot is echoed at the top so it is never buried

✓ Appointments

Date and time, type (clinic visit, monitoring scan, bloodwork, procedure or consultation), title, location, who it is with and notes — automatically sorted soonest-first with Upcoming and Past labels

✓ Monitoring & Lab Log

Date, type (bloodwork, scan or other), label, and the result notes exactly as your clinic reported them, plus your clinic's next-step notes. You record what you were told — the app does not interpret results

✓ Milestones

Stimulation start, egg retrieval, embryo report notes, transfer day and test day — each with a check-off, a date, and notes you can edit in place

✓ Two-Week-Wait Daily Journal

A short daily entry for the wait: day label, date, optional mood and your notes, kept in chronological order

✓ Costs Log

Item, amount, date, category and notes, with a live running total so you can see what the cycle has cost so far at a glance

✓ Printable Clinic Summary

A clean printable page that pulls together your key dates, trigger shot, medications table, upcoming appointments, milestones table and costs total — handy to print and take to an appointment
